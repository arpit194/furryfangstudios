Furry Fang Studios ("we", "us", "our") developed Infinite Tic Tac Toe (com.TwoDudesStudios.InfiniteTicTacToe) as a free game, available on Android. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

The app collects some information automatically as you use it, including:

- Your device's IP address
- The pages/screens you visit within the app, and how long you spend on them
- Your device's operating system

The app also collects your device's approximate location (derived from your IP address, not GPS), which we use to:

- Provide location-based features and recommendations within the app
- Analyze aggregated, anonymized usage trends to improve the app over time

We may occasionally use the information above to contact you with important notices or updates about the app.

## Third-Party Services

Infinite Tic Tac Toe uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

- [Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en) — displays advertisements and may collect advertising identifiers, device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads.
- [Unity](https://unity3d.com/legal/privacy-policy) — the engine the app is built with, and (if enabled) its associated services.

Only aggregated, anonymized data is shared with these services to help us improve the app.

## How We Disclose Information

We may disclose the information described above:

- As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process
- When we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request
- With trusted service providers who work on our behalf, have no independent use of the information, and have agreed to handle it in line with this policy

## Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the app by uninstalling it, using the standard uninstall process for your device or app marketplace.

## Data Retention

We retain the information described above for as long as you use the app, and for a reasonable time afterward. To request deletion of data you've provided through the app, contact us at [furryfangstudios@gmail.com](mailto:furryfangstudios@gmail.com) and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

## Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly direct the app at, or collect personal information from, children under the age of 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take the necessary action.

## Security

We take reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural precautions to protect the information we process and maintain.
