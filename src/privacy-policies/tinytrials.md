AmberFang ("we", "us", "our") developed Tiny Trials (com.amberfang.tinytrials) as a free game, available on Android. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

Tiny Trials does not require account creation and does not operate its own servers. Any names or settings you enter (such as player names or preferences) are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us.

We do not collect, store, or have access to any personal information you enter into the app.

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Children's Privacy

Tiny Trials is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

## Data Retention

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, there is no data for us to retain or delete beyond what is stored locally on your device (which you can clear by uninstalling the app).
