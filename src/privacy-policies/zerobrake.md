Furry Fang Studios ("we", "us", "our") developed Zero Brake (com.furryfangstudios.zerobrake) as a free game, available on Android. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

Zero Brake does not require account creation and does not operate its own servers. Your progress (scores and records, coins, garage purchases, car paint and tuning, daily challenge progress) and your settings are stored on your device; the save file itself is never transmitted to us. Summaries of gameplay events are included in the usage statistics described under Google Firebase and Homa Games below.

The game reads your device's motion sensors (gyroscope and accelerometer) to steer the car when tilt steering is selected. These readings are used on the device in real time only; they are never stored or sent anywhere.

We do not collect your name, email address or any other information that identifies you directly. To understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, the game sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase, described below. While we test the game with our publishing partner Homa Games, it also uses the measurement services described under Homa Games below.

## Third-Party Services

Zero Brake uses **Google AdMob** (part of Google Mobile Ads) to display advertisements: optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to save a run) and occasional ads during breaks in play. AdMob may collect and process data automatically to serve ads, including but not limited to:

- Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
- Device information (device type, operating system version)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- App usage data related to ad interactions

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are personalized. You can review or change your choice at any time from the game's settings (**AD PRIVACY**).

Ads need an internet connection. Without one, the game is fully playable and no ads are shown.

### Google Firebase: Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config

Zero Brake uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how the game is played (for example, how long runs last, which features are used, and where new players stop playing) and to measure ads and our own marketing campaigns. It records:

- Gameplay events: runs started and finished (score, length, distance, top and average speed, coins earned, crashes, close calls and similar counts), what was hit in a crash, personal records beaten, garage purchases made with in-game coins and car customisation choices (colour, livery, underglow), ads offered, shown and watched and the rewards they gave, save-run choices, tutorial and daily challenge progress, menu choices (time of day, start area), settings (controls, graphics quality, traffic) and the average frame rate
- Information collected automatically by Firebase: app version, device model, operating system version, language, approximate location (country or region, derived from the IP address), times of first launch and of play sessions, and a random app instance identifier created by Firebase
- Where permitted, the Android Advertising ID, used to measure ads and ad campaigns

Zero Brake uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to receive crash reports, which contain technical details of the crash (the code location, device model, operating system and app version, free memory and storage) and a random installation identifier, so that we can fix problems.

Zero Brake uses **Firebase Remote Config** to adjust game settings, such as how often ads appear and the size of in-game rewards, without an app update. To deliver these settings, Firebase uses a random installation identifier and basic device information (app version, operating system, language and country).

This information does not include your name or contact details, is used only to improve the game and our marketing, and is never sold. It is processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Where the law requires consent (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), analytics follows the same choice you make in Google's consent message: if you decline, Firebase only sends anonymous counts without identifiers. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (**AD PRIVACY**). Offline, events wait on your device and are sent the next time you are online.

### Homa Games, Meta and Adjust: game testing and advertising measurement

We are testing Zero Brake with our publishing partner **Homa Games** (Homa Games SAS, Paris, France). For this, the game includes Homa's software kit, which uses the following services to measure how the game is played and how well its advertising works:

- **Homa Games analytics**: gameplay events (runs started and finished, coins earned and spent, garage purchases, menus and rewarded-ad offers), app version, device model, operating system, language, country, and random identifiers created for the game. Processed by Homa Games in accordance with [Homa's Privacy Policy](https://homagames.com/privacy/).
- **Meta (Facebook) SDK**: app events such as installs and app launches, the Android Advertising ID, device information and IP address, to measure our advertising on Facebook and Instagram. The game does not use Facebook login and we do not receive any of your Facebook data. Processed by Meta Platforms in accordance with [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).
- **Adjust**: install attribution (which advertisement, if any, led to the install), the Android Advertising ID, the App Set ID, device information and IP address, also used to detect advertising fraud. Processed by Adjust GmbH in accordance with [Adjust's Privacy Policy](https://www.adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy/).

These services do not receive your name or contact details, and the data is never sold. Where the law requires consent (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), they start only if you agree in Google's consent message to storing information on your device and to measuring ad performance; otherwise they stay switched off. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (**AD PRIVACY**), and you can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Device Backups

Android's own backup service may include the game's save data in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Google under your device's backup settings; we never receive this data.

## Children's Privacy

Zero Brake is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

## Data Retention

We do not run our own servers. Your save data stays on your device (you can clear it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage). Analytics event data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months and crash reports by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, after which they are deleted; only aggregated totals that cannot identify anyone are kept longer. Because this data is anonymous, we cannot look up or delete the data of a specific player; it is deleted automatically at the end of these periods, and uninstalling the game stops all collection. Homa Games, Meta and Adjust keep their data under their own retention policies, linked above; you can ask them, or us, to delete it.
